## Design requirements recorded from Tom's words (not decisions on placement)

| # | Requirement | Source | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| DR-01 | The Builder entry carries an **optional category context** (the lead-menu keys `matcha` · `ube` · `chai` · `tea` · `opening`): the context's drinks are shown first, every other drink is reachable by scrolling; no context = the opening set first. | Tom 2026-10-01: "אנשים שהגיעו מהקמפיין מאצ'ה … יקבלו רק את הסוגים של המאצ'ה ויוכל גם שם לגלול ולראות עוד סוגים אבל בדיפולט הוא יראה את המאצ'ה" | Discovery model (MB-D06); link format; DL-19 |
| DR-02 | **No repetition.** Where the PDF and the Builder coexist, each has one distinct job; the Builder never re-presents as a step what the lead already saw. | Tom 2026-10-01: "אני חושש שזה יעמיס על המסע, וטיפה יחזור על עצמו" | Screen count, first screen, copy |
| DR-04 | **The UX must explain itself.** Every element that carries a rule (coverage, round-up, free expansion, estimate) is explained in place, concisely, or it is missed; the millimetre UX pass happens after the model decisions. | Tom 2026-10-01: "אם זה לא יהיה מוסבר טוב ומונגש בצורה מאוד מתומצתת, יפה, פשוטה, אבל כן מובנת מאוד, זה יתפספס" | Spec sections 7–11 (screen flow, hierarchy, components, microinteractions, states) |
| DR-05 | **Return dates travel with the product.** A drink whose product is off shows `לא זמין כרגע` and, when the planner set `back_on` and it has not passed, `צפי d.M`; with a planner alternative, `בינתיים: <alternative>`. Same overlay, same rule as the ordering portal. | Tom 2026-10-01: "כאשר יש תאריך שהוא צפוי לחזור, זה צריך להופיע גם שם, כמו שהוא מופיע גם במערכת של ממשק ההזמנות" | Availability display; no planner-side change needed (verified: `item_availability.back_on date`, `<input type="date">`, `routes.ts:119`) |
| DR-06 | **The salesperson always sees the menu.** Every staff surface that mentions a Builder lead carries the menu itself (drink names, count, kit total), not a pointer: the task card's "למה עכשיו" line, the staff email/Telegram alert, the drawer block, the timeline event. | Tom 2026-10-01: "וגם הסוכן מכירות צריך לדעת מה היה בתפריט שלו" | IC-2 payloads carry names; IC-3 drawer block; task title + reason text |
| DR-03 | The Builder must work in all three candidate placements without redesign: as its own lead journey, as the first-message asset instead of the PDF, or between the PDF and the order. | MB-D01 deferred | Entry = personal link + context; identity behind the link; no dependency on a specific WhatsApp text |
