## The three facts that reorganise everything

1. **The bottleneck is answering, not acquiring.** `199` leads in `sales_core`, `141` still
   `new`, `130` never touched at all, `3` won — measured 2026-08-31. Meanwhile 153 paying
   customers carry `₪776,534`/yr of identified opportunity that nobody has been assigned.
   Five of the six workstreams make GT easier to find. Only #3 and #6 make GT answer.
   **Weight time accordingly.**

2. **One decision blocks four documents.** The four commercial questions — starter package
   contents and prices · delivery time in business days · discount tiers by consumption ·
   commitment and exclusivity — gate customer-facing copy in #2, #3, #5 and #6
   simultaneously. Nothing derives them. Until they land, every one of those conversations
   is a hand-off to Alex.

3. **The single largest identified opportunity has no collateral.** The withdrawn `MUZA`
   line left `₪192,147`/yr across 20 customers, and `UNRESOLVED U-014` records that GT has
   **no drink page and no preparation spec for any cocktail base**. Twenty conversations
   worth `₪192k` currently have nothing to send. #5 §6.E and #6 §6.B are the same decision.

## Standing rules across all six

- `SALES_CUSTOMER_OUTREACH_WRITE_ENABLED` is `false`. **No session sends anything to a
  customer or a lead.** Drafts, lists and dry runs only.
- factory-os core (`stock_ledger`, `balance_anchors`, `bom_*`, `items`, `components`) is
  untouchable from every one of these workstreams.
- Every session ends by stamping its own masterprompt `SHIPPED` / `SUPERSEDED by <path>` /
  `ABANDONED — why`, with evidence pointers. **An unstamped masterprompt is
  indistinguishable from a live one**, and the most likely bad outcome of a re-paste is
  re-running work that already shipped.
- Every session writes its state back to its artifact. The artifacts are the shared board;
  a session that finishes without updating one has left the next person with a stale map.
- Numbers in these documents are dated. Past their shelf life, re-verify before planning —
  each masterprompt's §2.5 is a runnable block that costs one paste.
